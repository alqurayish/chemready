How it works

  1. Upload

    Drop up to 50 SDS PDFs at once.

  2. Extract and check

    AI reads each SDS. Plain code checks every quote, CAS number and H code.

  3. Review what is doubtful

    You see only the flagged fields, next to the page they came from.

  4. Export your inventory

    Approved products become your chemical inventory list, ready for Excel.
